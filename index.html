✳ THE IDEA

Small distances. Big adventures.

This little calculator estimates how long it takes to change lines in the Barcelona metro. Enter how far you walk, and how many steps or metres the stairs, escalators and moving walkways add to your route.

THE SIMPLE MATHS

Walking ÷ 1.3 m/s
Stairs ÷ 1.9 steps/s
Escalator ÷ 1.5 steps/s
Walkway ÷ 0.5 m/s

Times are added together to estimate your transfer.
